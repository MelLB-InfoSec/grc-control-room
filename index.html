GRC Control Room

A practical governance, risk and compliance operating model designed to show how a modern GRC programme can translate responsibilities into repeatable workflows, accountable ownership, measurable progress and audit-ready evidence.

The problem

GRC work is often fragmented across spreadsheets, tickets, evidence requests and stakeholder follow-ups. This prototype brings the operating rhythm into one structured view.

What this demonstrates

Risk management, audit readiness, third-party risk, access governance, control monitoring, policy operations, regulatory tracking and control-owner enablement.
